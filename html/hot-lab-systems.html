02Hot Lab Workflow

Making Invisible Work Visible

The Hot Lab functions as the heavy-duty operational hub of Nuclear Medicine, coordinating and managing critical activities across all examinations.

I identified that essential Hot Lab work—including patient preparation and dose administration—remained undocumented and unrecognized in staff productivity, then designed dedicated EPIC workflow steps to capture each activity and its duration within the examination.
