Structured Multi-Visit Architecture

EPIC Workflow

One clinical order remains connected to every required visit while scheduling, attendance, documentation, and workload remain visible at visit level.

Patient Safety

Structured multi-visit workflow reduces missed appointments and supports continuity of care.

Scheduling Model

Multi-visit scheduling under a single clinical order.

Visit Management

Individual tracking for completed, current, scheduled, and pending visits.

Clinical Documentation

Separate appointment confirmation and attendance documentation for every visit.

Staff Productivity

Every visit is individually recorded, accurately reflecting the department's actual workload.

Implemented Workflow Observation

SAP Workflow

The implemented process represented the full multi-day study as one registration, leaving the individual return visits outside a visit-level scheduling trail.

Patient Risk

Reliance on patient memory may lead to missed follow-up appointments.

Scheduling Model

Single registration maintained for the entire study.

Visit Management

No visit-by-visit scheduling within the implemented workflow.

Clinical Documentation

No separate appointment or attendance proof for each visit.

Staff Productivity

Multiple days of clinical work are represented as a single encounter, limiting accurate workload reporting.
