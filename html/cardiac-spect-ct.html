01Before

Conventional Cardiac SPECT

Conventional myocardial perfusion imaging could require additional views to address breast and diaphragmatic attenuation artifacts.

Extra Images → More Examination Time

More time for the patient on the imaging table.

02The Transformation

Introducing Cardiac SPECT/CT

Cardiac imaging was transitioned to SPECT/CT, integrating CT-based soft-tissue attenuation correction into the myocardial perfusion workflow.

Soft-Tissue Attenuation Correction

Breast artifact ✕ • Diaphragm artifact ✕ • No extra views

The Result

Fused NM + CT Imaging. Computed Attenuation Correction. More Accurate Results. Less Imaging Time.

The new SPECT/CT modality combined fused NM and CT imaging with computed attenuation correction, improving diagnostic accuracy while reducing imaging time for the patient.
